## Note
**The partnership (p. 10).** The IRS describes itself as a proud partner of the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children® (NCMEC). Photographs of missing children chosen by the Center may be printed in the publication on pages that would otherwise be blank.

**How to help (p. 10).** Readers can help bring these children home by looking at the photographs and, if they recognize a child, calling 1-800-THE-LOST (1-800-843-5678).
