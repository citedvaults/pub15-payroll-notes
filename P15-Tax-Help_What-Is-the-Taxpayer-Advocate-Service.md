## Note
The Taxpayer Advocate Service (TAS) is an independent organization within the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) (p. 58). TAS helps taxpayers resolve problems with the IRS, makes administrative and legislative recommendations to prevent or correct the problems, and protects taxpayer rights (p. 58). It works to ensure that every taxpayer is treated fairly and that taxpayers know and understand their rights under the Taxpayer Bill of Rights (p. 58). The page adds the line "We are Your Voice at the IRS" (p. 58).
