## Note
A deposit that falls due on a day that is not a business day is timely if it is made by the close of the next business day. Any day except a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday is a business day. For example, a deposit due on a Friday that is a legal holiday is timely if it is made the following Monday, provided that Monday is a business day.

A semiweekly schedule depositor has at least 3 business days after the close of the semiweekly period in which to deposit. Each legal holiday among the 3 weekdays after the end of a semiweekly period adds one more day for the required deposit. For example, with taxes accumulated on payments made on a Friday and the following Monday a legal holiday, the deposit normally due on Wednesday may be made on Thursday, which still leaves 3 business days.

**Legal holiday.** The term means any legal holiday in the District of Columbia; for the deposit rules it leaves out other statewide legal holidays. The 2026 legal holidays are:
- New Year’s Day, January 1
- Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr., January 19
- Washington’s Birthday, February 16
- District of Columbia Emancipation Day, April 16
- Memorial Day, May 25
- Juneteenth National Independence Day, June 19
- Independence Day (observed), July 3
- Labor Day, September 7
- Indigenous Peoples’ Day (Columbus Day), October 12
- Veterans Day, November 11
- Thanksgiving Day, November 26
- Christmas Day, December 25
