## Note
This part of section 17 lays out the steps federal agencies must take to certify to the IRS how much "federal income taxes" were paid to, and deposited into, the U.S. Treasury (p. 55).

**Who certifies, and when (pp. 55–56).** Every department and agency of the federal government, and every service and social organization associated with a military or civilian federal entity, that withholds federal income taxes on payments to employees and pensioners of the United States (or an agency of it) as the section provides must certify to the IRS each calendar quarter. The certification gives the total federal income taxes withheld that have been deposited into the U.S. Treasury. A separate certification is required for federal income taxes creditable to American Samoa, the CNMI and Guam, as applicable (p. 56).

**What the certification contains (p. 56).** Unless the section provides otherwise, a certification should be a letter that includes:
1. Pub. 15, cited as the authority for the certification;
2. the name of the federal agency or department that certifies;
3. that agency's EIN;
4. the calendar quarter and fiscal year covered;
5. how many individuals the certification covers; and
6. the aggregate dollar amount of federal income taxes withheld on all those individuals.

**Certification for other agencies (p. 56).** A federal department or agency that certifies for another department or agency must list the name and EIN of each subordinate or designated federal department or agency it covers, with the required data for each. That certifying agency must send its certification at least once a year, by February 14 at the latest.

**Military personnel not stationed in the territories (p. 56).** Federal agencies that certify for pay of military personnel who are not stationed in American Samoa, the CNMI or Guam, but whose state of legal residence is one of these territories, must further provide each servicemember's name, SSN, amount of annual salary paid, and total amount of annual federal income tax withheld.

**Agreement with the quarterly return (p. 56).** The amounts in the certification must agree with the federal income tax withheld that the agency reports on its quarterly federal tax return(s) (Form 941).

**Quarterly due dates (p. 56).** Certifications are due quarterly, within 45 days after each calendar quarter closes, on these dates:
- May 15 for the first quarter, which ends March 31;
- Aug. 14 for the second quarter, which ends June 30;
- Nov. 14 for the third quarter, which ends September 30;
- Feb. 14 for the fourth quarter, which ends December 31.

**Where to mail it (p. 56).** Federal agencies should mail the certification to the Internal Revenue Service, Revenue Systems and Analysis, at the address the page prints: Attn: OS:CFO:FM:RA:S (77K St), 1111 Constitution Ave NW, CFO/FM - Mail Stop 6167, Washington, DC 20224. The page also lists the fax number 202-803-9691.
