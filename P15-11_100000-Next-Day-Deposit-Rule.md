## Note
Once taxes of $100,000 or more accumulate on any day of a monthly or semiweekly deposit period, they must be deposited by the next business day, whether you are a monthly or a semiweekly schedule depositor. The $100,000 threshold for a next-day deposit is measured before any reduction of the liability for nonrefundable credits (p. 34).

For this rule, accumulation does not carry on past the end of a deposit period. Example: a semiweekly schedule depositor has $95,000 accumulated by a Tuesday (in a Saturday-through-Tuesday deposit period) and accumulates a further $10,000 on the Wednesday. The next-day rule does not apply, since the $10,000 falls in the next deposit period; the $95,000 is deposited by Friday and the $10,000 by the following Wednesday (pp. 34–35).

Conversely, once at least $100,000 has accumulated in a deposit period, accumulation stops at the end of that day and starts afresh the next day. Example: Fir Co., a semiweekly schedule depositor, accumulates $110,000 on Monday and must deposit it on Tuesday, the next business day. On Tuesday it accumulates another $30,000. That amount is not added to the $110,000 and is under $100,000, so Fir Co. deposits it by Friday under the semiweekly schedule (p. 35).

**Caution.** A monthly schedule depositor that accumulates a $100,000 tax liability on any day of the deposit period turns into a semiweekly schedule depositor from the next day, and stays one for at least the remainder of the calendar year and for the following calendar year (p. 35).

**Example (p. 35).** Elm, Inc., began business on May 4, 2026. It paid wages for the first time on Wednesday, May 6, accumulating a $40,000 liability, and paid wages again on Friday, May 8, accumulating $60,000, which brought its total to $100,000. In its first year the lookback liability is treated as zero, so the lookback rules alone would make it a monthly schedule depositor. But because $100,000 accumulated on May 8, it became a semiweekly schedule depositor on May 9, and it remains one for the rest of 2026 and for 2027. It is required to deposit the $100,000 by Monday, May 11, the next business day.
