## Note
Part-time workers and workers hired for short periods of time are treated the same as full-time employees for federal income tax withholding and for social security, Medicare and FUTA tax purposes (p. 31).

Generally, it doesn't matter whether the part-time worker, or the worker hired for a short period of time, has another job or has the maximum amount of social security tax withheld by another employer. The page refers to Successor employer, earlier in section 9, for an exception to this rule (p. 31).

For income tax withholding, the full-time method may be used, or the part-year employment method that section 6 of Pub. 15-T explains (p. 31).
