## Note
Code sections 931(a), 931(d) and 7654 require the U.S. Government to transfer ("cover over") federal income taxes to the Treasury of American Samoa. These are the taxes withheld on payments to military and civilian employees and to pensioners who are residents of American Samoa (p. 54). The federal government moves the federal income taxes withheld or collected from its employees and pensioners who are residents of American Samoa to the American Samoa Treasury at least once a year (p. 54).

To let the federal government cover over these taxes as the law requires, federal agencies must certify the federal income taxes withheld or collected from their employees and pensioners, following the procedures under Certification Procedures, later in the section (p. 54).

**Related:** [[P15-17_Certification-Procedures]] — the procedures federal agencies follow to certify to the IRS the federal income taxes paid to and deposited into the U.S. Treasury (p. 55)
