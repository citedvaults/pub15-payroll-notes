## Note
**Which address applies (p. 9).** The address for filing Form 940, Form 941, Form 943, Form 944, Form 945 or Form CT-1 generally turns on where your residence or principal place of business is located, and on whether a payment goes with the return.

**Tax-exempt organizations and government entities (pp. 9–10).** A tax-exempt organization or a government entity has separate filing addresses for these returns. The page refers to the separate instructions for each of the forms for the addresses.

**Related:** [[P15-Private-Delivery-Services-PDSs]] — private delivery services designated by the IRS for the timely mailing as timely filing rule, and the IRS mailing address to use with one (p. 10)
