## Note
**The penalty (p. 10).** A payment in any form that a financial institution dishonors and returns draws a penalty of $25 or 2% of the payment, whichever is more.

**Small payments (p. 10).** For a dishonored payment of $24.99 or less, though, the penalty equals the amount of the payment. A dishonored payment of $18, for example, is charged a penalty of $18.
