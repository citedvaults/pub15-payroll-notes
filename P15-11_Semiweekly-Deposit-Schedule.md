## Note
A Form 941 filer is a semiweekly schedule depositor for a calendar year when the Form 941 line 12 taxes for the lookback period come to more than $50,000. A filer of Form 943, Form 944 or Form 945 is a semiweekly schedule depositor for the calendar year when the lookback-period taxes, taken from line 13 of Form 943, line 9 of Form 944 or line 3 of Form 945, were more than $50,000.

The semiweekly schedule sets the deposit date by the day of the payment. Employment taxes on payments made on Wednesday, Thursday and/or Friday are deposited by the following Wednesday, and those on payments made on Saturday, Sunday, Monday and/or Tuesday by the following Friday. The page points ahead to Deposits Due on Business Days Only.

Table 2. Semiweekly Deposit Schedule (p. 33)

| IF the payday falls on a... | THEN deposit taxes by the following... |
|---|---|
| Wednesday, Thursday, and/or Friday | Wednesday. |
| Saturday, Sunday, Monday, and/or Tuesday | Friday. |

**Caution.** A semiweekly schedule depositor must complete Schedule B (Form 941) and submit it with Form 941. A Form 943 filer in that position completes Form 943-A and submits it with Form 943, and a Form 944 or Form 945 filer completes Form 945-A and submits it with the return.

**Deposit period spanning 2 quarters (Form 941 filers).** More than 1 pay date within a semiweekly period, with the pay dates in different calendar quarters, means separate deposits for the separate liabilities. For example, pay dates of Wednesday, September 30, 2026 (third quarter) and Friday, October 2, 2026 (fourth quarter) call for two separate deposits although they share one semiweekly period, and both deposits are due Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

**Deposit period spanning 2 return periods (Form 943, Form 944 or Form 945 filers).** The period a return covers is its return period, and for the annual Forms 943, 944 and 945 that is a calendar year. When more than 1 pay date falls in one semiweekly period but in different return periods, separate deposits are needed for the separate liabilities. For example, if a return period ends on a Thursday, the taxes accumulated on Wednesday and Thursday make up one deposit obligation and the taxes accumulated on Friday a separate one, because two different return periods are involved.

**Steps to determine your deposit schedule.**
1. Identify the lookback period, as explained earlier in the section.
2. Add up the taxes reported for the lookback period on Form 941 line 12, Form 943 line 13, Form 944 line 9 or Form 945 line 3.
3. Decide from the total whether you are a monthly or a semiweekly schedule depositor, using the table below.

| Lookback-period total | You are a... |
|---|---|
| $50,000 or less | monthly schedule depositor. |
| more than $50,000 | semiweekly schedule depositor. |
