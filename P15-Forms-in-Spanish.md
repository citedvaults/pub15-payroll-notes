## Note
**Spanish-language versions (p. 7).** Spanish-language versions are available, for employers and for employees, of many of the forms and instructions the publication discusses. Examples are Form W-4 (sp), Form W-9 (sp), Form SS-4 (sp), Form 941 (sp) and Form 944 (sp).

**Not every instance is referenced (p. 7).** The publication does not mention a Spanish-language form or instruction each time one is available. To find out whether a Spanish-language version exists, the page refers to Pub. 15 (sp) and to IRS.gov/SpanishForms.

**Related:** [[P15-Hiring-New-Employees]] — verifying that each new employee is legally eligible to work, new hire reporting and recording a new employee’s name and SSN (p. 7)
