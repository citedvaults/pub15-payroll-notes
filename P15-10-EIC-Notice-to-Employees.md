## Note
**Who must be notified (p. 31).** You must notify employees who have no federal income tax withheld that they may be able to claim a tax refund because of the EIC. This does not apply to employees in American Samoa, Guam, Puerto Rico, the CNMI and the USVI. Notifying employees who claim exemption from withholding on Form W-4 about the EIC is not required. Notifying any employee whose wages for 2025 were less than $61,555 ($68,675 if married filing jointly) is encouraged, so that they know they may be eligible to claim the credit for 2025; the page gives the reason that eligible employees may get a refund of the amount of the EIC that exceeds the tax they owe.

**How to meet the requirement (p. 31).** Issuing the employee a Form W-2 with the EIC notice printed on the back of Copy B meets the notification requirement, as does a substitute Form W-2 carrying the same statement. Providing Notice 797, Possible Federal Tax Refund Due to the Earned Income Credit (EIC), or a statement of your own with the same wording, also meets it.

**Timing (p. 31).**
- A substitute for Form W-2 may be given on time without the required statement. You must then notify the employee within 1 week of the date the substitute was given.
- Form W-2 may be required but not given on time. You must then give the employee Notice 797 or your written statement by the date Form W-2 is required to be given.
- Form W-2 may not be required. You must then notify the employee by February 9, 2026.
