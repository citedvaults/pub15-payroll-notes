## Note
Employment taxes, Form 945 taxes included, must be deposited by EFT. For the cases in which tax may be paid with the return in place of a deposit, the page points back to Payment with return (p. 35).

**Electronic deposit requirement (p. 35).** Every federal tax deposit must be made by EFT, which can be done through EFTPS, IRS Direct Pay or your IRS business tax account. If you prefer not to use one of these, you can arrange for your tax professional, financial institution, payroll service or another trusted third party to make the electronic deposits for you. EFTPS is a free service of the Department of the Treasury, and payments through IRS Direct Pay or the IRS business tax account are free as well. For more on IRS Direct Pay and on payments through the business tax account, the page gives IRS.gov/Pay; for more on EFTPS or to enroll, it gives EFTPS.gov and telephone numbers, among them numbers for reaching EFTPS through TRS. Pub. 966 has further information on EFTPS.

**When you receive your EIN (p. 35).** A new employer that indicated a federal tax obligation when requesting an EIN is pre-enrolled in EFTPS. The EIN Package carries information about Express Enrollment, and a further mailing brings the EFTPS personal identification number (PIN) with instructions for activating it. The enrollment is activated by calling the toll-free number in the “How to Activate Your Enrollment” brochure, after which payroll tax deposits can begin. An employer that outsources payroll and related tax duties to a third-party payer, such as a payroll service provider (PSP) or reporting agent, should tell that payer about the EFTPS enrollment.

**Deposit record (p. 35).** Each successful payment gets an EFT Trace Number, which can serve as a receipt or be used to trace the payment.

**Depositing on time (p. 36).** EFTPS accepts same-day payments of $1 million or less when they are submitted before 3:00 p.m. Eastern time on a business day. A payment above $1 million has to be submitted by 8:00 p.m. Eastern time on the day before the deposit is due. A third party making the deposit for you may set different cutoff times.

**Same-day wire payment option (p. 36).** If a timely deposit transaction wasn’t submitted on EFTPS, the Federal Tax Collection Service (FTCS) can still be used for a same-day wire payment that makes the deposit on time. This needs arrangements with your financial institution in advance; availability, deadlines and costs are to be checked with it, and it may charge a fee. The page gives IRS.gov/SameDayWire for the information to give your financial institution.

**Claiming credit for overpayments (p. 36).** Where more than the right amount of taxes was deposited for a quarter, the choice between a refund and a credit toward the next return is made on Form 941 for that quarter (or on Form 943, Form 944 or Form 945 for the year). EFTPS is not to be asked to request a refund from the IRS.
