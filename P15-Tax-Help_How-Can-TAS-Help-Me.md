## Note
TAS can help with problems that you have not been able to resolve with the IRS on your own (p. 58). The page says to always try to resolve the problem with the IRS first and, if that fails, to come to TAS. Its services are free (p. 58).
- TAS serves all taxpayers and their representatives, whether individuals, businesses or exempt organizations. Eligibility for TAS help may arise if an IRS problem is causing you financial difficulty, if you tried to resolve your issue with the IRS and could not, or if you believe an IRS system, process or procedure just is not working as it should (p. 58).
- For help any time with general tax topics, the page gives the TAS website, www.TaxpayerAdvocate.IRS.gov. The site can help with common tax issues and situations, such as what to do if you make a mistake on your return or get a notice from the IRS (p. 58).
- TAS works to resolve large-scale (systemic) problems that affect many taxpayers. Systemic issues can be reported at www.IRS.gov/SAMS, and the page asks that no personal identifiable information be included (p. 58).
