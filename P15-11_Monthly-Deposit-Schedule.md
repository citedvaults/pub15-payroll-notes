## Note
A Form 941 filer is a monthly schedule depositor for a calendar year when the Form 941 line 12 taxes of the 4 quarters in the lookback period add up to $50,000 or less. A filer of Form 943, Form 944 or Form 945 is a monthly schedule depositor for the calendar year when the lookback-period taxes, taken from line 13 of Form 943, line 9 of Form 944 or line 3 of Form 945, were $50,000 or less.

The monthly schedule calls for employment taxes on payments made in a month to be deposited by the 15th day of the next month. The page points ahead to Deposits Due on Business Days Only and to the $100,000 Next-Day Deposit Rule. A monthly schedule depositor should not file Form 941, Form 943, Form 944 or Form 945 every month.

**New employers.** For a Form 941 filer, tax liability for any lookback-period quarter that came before the business was started or acquired counts as zero, which makes the filer a monthly schedule depositor in the first calendar year of the business. For a filer of Form 943, Form 944 or Form 945, liability in the lookback period before the start or acquisition of the business likewise counts as zero, which gives monthly schedule depositor status in the first and second calendar years. The page then says to see the $100,000 Next-Day Deposit Rule, later in the section.
