## Note
**Tax questions (p. 10).** Employment tax questions can be put to the IRS Business and Specialty Tax Line by calling 800-829-4933.

**Help for people with disabilities (p. 10).** Persons who are deaf, hard of hearing or have a speech disability may call 800-829-4059 (TDD/TTY) with any employment tax questions. That number may also be used for assistance with unresolved tax problems.

**Additional information (p. 10).** The page points to IRS.gov/EmploymentTaxes for additional employment tax information, to IRS.gov/AgricultureTaxCenter for general tax information relevant to agricultural employers, and to IRS.gov/ACA for information about employer responsibilities under the Affordable Care Act.
