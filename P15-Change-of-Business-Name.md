## Note
**Notifying the IRS (p. 9).** Notify the IRS immediately if you change your business name. To do so, write to the IRS office where you file your returns, using the Without a payment address given in the instructions for your employment tax return, to report any business name change.

**New EIN (p. 9).** The page refers to Pub. 1635 to see whether you need to apply for a new EIN.

**Related:** [[P15-Change-of-Address-or-Responsible-Party]] — notifying the IRS immediately of a change of business address or responsible party (p. 9)
