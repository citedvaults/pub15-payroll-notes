## Note
**Notifying the IRS (p. 9).** A change of business address or responsible party must be reported to the IRS immediately. Form 8822-B, completed and mailed, is how the IRS is told of either change.

**Responsible party (p. 9).** For what “responsible party” means, the page refers to the Instructions for Form SS-4.
