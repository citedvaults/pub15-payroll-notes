## Note
**Who needs an EIN (p. 13).** You need an EIN if you are required to report employment taxes or to give tax statements to employees or annuitants. The EIN is a nine-digit number that the IRS issues, arranged as 00-0000000. It identifies the tax accounts of employers and of certain others who have no employees. Use your EIN on all of the items you send to the IRS and the SSA. The page points to Pub. 1635 for more information.

**Applying for an EIN (p. 13).** If you don't have an EIN, you may apply for one online at IRS.gov/EIN. You may also apply by faxing or mailing Form SS-4 to the IRS. If the principal business was created or organized outside the United States or U.S. territories, you may also apply by calling 267-941-1099 (a toll call). Don't use an SSN in place of an EIN.

**Only one EIN (p. 13).** You should have only one EIN. Should you hold more than one and be unsure which applies, call 800-829-4933, or 800-829-4059 (TDD/TTY, for persons who are deaf, hard of hearing or have a speech disability). Give each number you hold, the name and address tied to each, and the address of your main place of business, and the IRS will say which number to use. The page points to Pub. 1635 for more information.

**Successor employers and a pending EIN (p. 13).** If you took over another employer's business (the page refers to Successor employer in section 9), don't use that employer's EIN. If you have applied for an EIN but don't have it by the time a return is due, file a paper return and enter "Applied For" and the date you applied in the space shown for the number.

**Caution: the EIN must match (pp. 13–14).** The EIN on any form you file has to be identical to the EIN the IRS assigned to your business. An SSN or ITIN must not be used on forms that ask for an EIN. Suppose the EIN you used on Form 941, Form 943 or Form 944 (a prior owner's EIN included) differs from the one reported on Form W-3: the page then refers to Box h—Other EIN used this year in the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3. For Puerto Rico's Form W-3 (PR), "Other EIN used this year" is reported in box f. Form 945 must show the same name and EIN as your information returns that report federal income tax withholding (for example, backup withholding reported on Form 1099-NEC). A Form 945 filed with an incorrect EIN, or with another business's EIN, may bring penalties and delays in processing the return.

**Agricultural employers that have crew leaders (p. 14).** An agricultural employer must record the crew leader's name, address and EIN. The page refers to sections 2 and 14.
