## Note
Code section 7654 and 48 U.S.C. section 1421(h) require the U.S. Government to cover over federal income taxes to the Treasury of Guam. These are the taxes withheld on payments to military and civilian employees and to pensioners who are residents of Guam (p. 55). The federal government transfers the federal income taxes withheld or collected from its employees and pensioners who are residents of Guam to the Guam Treasury at least once a year (p. 55).

To let the federal government cover over these taxes as the law requires, federal agencies must certify the federal income taxes withheld or collected from their employees, following the procedures under Certification Procedures, later in the section (p. 55).

**Related:** [[P15-17_Certification-Procedures]] — the procedures federal agencies follow to certify to the IRS the federal income taxes paid to and deposited into the U.S. Treasury (p. 55)
