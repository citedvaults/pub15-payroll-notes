## Note
This part of section 17 describes which "federal income taxes" are subject to the certification procedures (p. 55). For the purposes of the cover over certification requirements, the term "federal income taxes" includes federal income taxes that were withheld from compensation and other amounts paid to any of the following groups and deposited into the U.S. Treasury (p. 55):
- Federal government civilian employees who are residents of American Samoa, the CNMI or Guam.
- Recipients (including survivors) of federal pensions, civilian or military, who are residents of American Samoa, the CNMI or Guam.
- Military personnel stationed in American Samoa, the CNMI or Guam.
- Military personnel not stationed in American Samoa, the CNMI or Guam who have a state of legal residence in any of these territories.
- Employees of a service or social organization associated with a military or civilian agency in American Samoa, the CNMI or Guam.
