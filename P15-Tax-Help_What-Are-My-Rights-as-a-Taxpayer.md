## Note
The Taxpayer Bill of Rights describes ten basic rights that all taxpayers have when dealing with the IRS (p. 58). The page gives www.TaxpayerAdvocate.IRS.gov/Taxpayer-Rights for more information about the rights, what they mean and how they apply to specific situations that may come up with the IRS (p. 58). TAS strives to protect taxpayer rights and to ensure that the IRS administers the tax law in a fair and equitable way (p. 58).
