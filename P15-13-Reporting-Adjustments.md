## Note
Section 13 of the publication covers reporting adjustments to Form 941, Form 943 and Form 944. It has three parts, Current Period Adjustments, Prior-Period Adjustments and Wage Repayments, which follow in the cluster below. The chapter's own text is its heading alone, so nothing further is summarised here.

## Parts of Reporting Adjustments
- [[P15-13_Current-Period-Adjustments]] — adjustments to social security and Medicare taxes for the current period: fractions of cents, third-party sick pay, tips and group-term life insurance premiums for former employees, with an example and the rule that the record of federal tax liability does not change (pp. 40–41)
- [[P15-13_Prior-Period-Adjustments]] — correcting errors found on previously filed returns with Form 941-X, Form 943-X or Form 944-X, the adjustment and claim processes, income tax and Additional Medicare Tax withholding adjustments, collecting and refunding amounts withheld wrongly, corrected Forms W-2 and W-3, and exceptions to interest-free corrections (pp. 41–43)
- [[P15-13_Wage-Repayments]] — what to do when an employee repays wages received in error, for repayments of current-year and of prior-year wages, and how the employee reports a repayment (p. 43)
