## Note
Code section 7654 and 48 U.S.C. section 1681 (P.L. 94-241, section 703(b)) require the U.S. Government to cover over federal income taxes to the CNMI Treasury. These are the taxes withheld on payments to military and civilian employees and to pensioners who are residents of the CNMI (pp. 54–55). The federal government transfers the federal income taxes withheld or collected from those employees and pensioners to the CNMI Treasury at least once a year (p. 55).

To let the federal government cover over these taxes as the law requires, federal agencies must certify the federal income taxes withheld or collected from their employees and pensioners, following the procedures under Certification Procedures, later in the section (p. 55). Federal agencies are not required to certify local CNMI taxes that are withheld or collected, as the Caution next to it explains (p. 55).

**Caution (p. 55).** In December 2006 the U.S. Treasury Department and the CNMI Division of Revenue and Taxation made an agreement under 5 U.S.C. section 5517. Under it, every federal employer (the Department of Defense included) must withhold CNMI income taxes in place of federal income taxes, and deposit them with the CNMI Treasury, for employees whose regular place of federal employment is the CNMI. Federal employers must also file quarterly and annual reports with the CNMI Division of Revenue and Taxation. The 5517 agreement does not apply to payments to pensioners, or to pay of members of the U.S. Armed Forces who are stationed in the CNMI but whose state of legal residence is outside the CNMI. The page refers to IRS.gov/5517Agreements for more information, including details on completing Form W-2.
