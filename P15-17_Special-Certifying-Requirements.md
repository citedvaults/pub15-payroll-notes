## Note
This part of section 17 gives the legal authorities under which federal agencies must certify to the IRS how much federal income tax was withheld from payments to U.S. Government employees who work in American Samoa, the CNMI and Guam, and to federal civilian and military pensioners who reside there (p. 54). The page says that these special certifying requirements do not apply to federal agencies that have employees working in Puerto Rico or the USVI (p. 54).
