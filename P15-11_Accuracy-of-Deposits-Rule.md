## Note
The requirement is to deposit the whole, 100%, of the tax liability on or before the deposit due date. No penalty is applied for depositing less than 100% provided both of these conditions are met:
- The shortfall is no more than the greater of $100 or 2% of the taxes otherwise required to be deposited.
- The shortfall is paid or deposited by the shortfall makeup date described below.

**Shortfall makeup date.**
1. Monthly schedule depositor: deposit the shortfall, or pay it with the return, by the due date of the return for the return period in which the shortfall occurred. The shortfall may be paid with the return even when the amount is $2,500 or more.
2. Semiweekly schedule depositor: the shortfall is deposited by the earlier of two dates. One (a) is the first Wednesday or Friday, whichever comes first, on or after the 15th day of the month following the month of the shortfall; the other (b) is the due date of the return for the return period of the tax liability.

**Example.** For a semiweekly schedule depositor with a deposit shortfall during April 2026, the makeup date is Friday, May 15, 2026. If instead the shortfall occurred on the required deposit due date of Wednesday, July 1, 2026, for the pay date of Friday, June 26, 2026, the return due date for that pay date, July 31, 2026, comes before the makeup date of Wednesday, August 19, 2026, so the shortfall must be deposited by July 31, 2026.
