## Note
**Filing and paying electronically (p. 6).** Businesses can benefit from filing and paying federal taxes electronically, and the IRS offers convenient and secure programs for this, whether a tax professional is relied on or the business handles its own taxes. For e-file the page points to IRS.gov/EmploymentEfile and says a fee may be charged to file electronically. For electronic payment options it points to IRS.gov/Pay.

**Electronic filing of Forms W-2 (p. 6).** The page points to SSA.gov/employer for electronic filing of Forms W-2 (Forms W-2AS, W-2CM, W-2GU, W-2VI and Forms 499R-2/W-2PR included). Electronic filing of Forms W-2 may be required, and the page refers to the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3. Anyone with problems filing electronically can contact the SSA at 800-772-6270. The SSA’s Regional Employer Services Liaison Officer can be reached through SSA.gov/employer/wage_reporting_specialists.htm, and these officers can help with all questions about the SSA’s payroll reporting processes and applications.

**Employers in the CNMI and Puerto Rico (p. 6).** CNMI employers are told to contact their local tax department for instructions on completing Form W-2CM; the form and its instructions are at Finance.gov.mp/forms.php or by calling 670-664-1000. The SSA’s Business Services Online (BSO) is independent of the Government of Puerto Rico’s electronic filing system, and Puerto Rico employers must go to Hacienda.pr.gov for additional information.

**Caution: a valid EIN is required (p. 6).** Whenever a return is filed or a federal tax payment made electronically, a valid employer identification number (EIN) must be provided at that time. A return or payment submitted without a valid EIN won’t be processed, and penalties may follow. The page refers to section 1 for applying for an EIN.

**Electronic funds withdrawal (EFW) (pp. 6–7).** An employment tax return that is e-filed can be paid, as to the balance due, in the same step by EFW, through tax preparation software or a tax professional. EFW must not be used for federal tax deposits. The page points to IRS.gov/EFW.

**Credit or debit card payments (p. 7).** The balance due shown on an employment tax return can be paid by credit or debit card. A payment processor handles the payment and charges a processing fee. A credit or debit card must not be used for federal tax deposits. The page points to IRS.gov/PayByCard.

**Online payment agreement (p. 7).** If you can’t pay the full tax you owe when you file your employment tax return, you may be eligible to apply online for an installment agreement. The page refers to the instructions for your employment tax return and to IRS.gov/OPA.
