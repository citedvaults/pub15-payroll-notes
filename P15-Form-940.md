## Note
- Return transcripts for Forms 940, 941, 943, 944 and 945 for tax years 2023 and later are now available through your IRS business tax account (F03, p. 3).
- An amended Form 940, Employer’s Annual Federal Unemployment (FUTA) Tax Return, can be filed electronically using Modernized e-File (MeF) (F04, p. 3).
- Section 3504 agents and CPEOs that the IRS has approved must complete Schedule R (Form 940) when they file an aggregate Form 940 (F04, p. 5).
- Aggregate Forms 940 may be filed by agents for recipients of home care services provided through a government-administered program (federal, state or local), and by CPEOs the IRS approved under section 7705 (F04, p. 5).
- CPEOs notify the IRS on Form 8973 when a service contract with a client or customer starts or ends, and generally must file Form 940 and Schedule R (Form 940) electronically (F04, p. 5).
- Without a “final” Form 940 and a “final” Form 941, Form 943 or Form 944, you keep filing Form 940 even for periods with no wages paid (F04, p. 6).
- The IRS encourages filing a “zero wage” Form 940 electronically (F04, p. 6).
- The Employer Responsibilities list names filing Form 940 among the items under Annually (see Calendar for due dates) (F09, p. 8).
- The filing address for Form 940 generally turns on where your residence or principal place of business is located and on whether a payment goes with the return (F14, p. 9).
- Tax-exempt organizations and government entities have separate filing addresses, which are in the separate instructions for Form 940 (F14, pp. 9–10).
- Under the Calendar’s By January 31 date, Form 940 reports any FUTA tax; if you deposited all of that tax when due, you may file by February 10 (F21, p. 11).
- FUTA tax is paid by the employer alone and isn’t withheld from the employee’s wages; the page points to the Instructions for Form 940 for more information (S14, p. 43).
- The credit for amounts paid into state unemployment funds is determined using the Instructions for Form 940 (S14, p. 44).
- Where certain states leave some wage types out of state unemployment tax although FUTA tax covers them, you may have to deposit more than 0.6% FUTA tax on those wages, and the page points to the Instructions for Form 940 for further guidance (S14, p. 44).
- An employer that acquired a business from a FUTA-liable employer may be able to count that employer’s wages toward the $7,000 wage base limit; the page points to the Instructions for Form 940 (S14, p. 44).
- The quarterly FUTA liability is figured at 0.6% of taxable wages, and may need adjusting depending on the credit for state unemployment contributions; the page points to the Instructions for Form 940 (S14, p. 44).
- If the fourth-quarter FUTA liability (plus any undeposited amount from an earlier quarter) is over $500, the whole amount is deposited by Form 940’s due date (January 31); at $500 or less you may deposit, pay by credit or debit card, or pay with Form 940 by January 31 (S14, p. 44).
- A Form 940 filed electronically can be paid with EFW for any balance due (S14, p. 44).
- In years with credit reduction states, the liabilities owed for credit reduction belong with the fourth quarter deposit, and the page points to the Instructions for Form 940 (S14, p. 44).
- Form 940 is the form for reporting FUTA tax. Your 2025 Form 940 is due by February 2, 2026, or on or before February 10, 2026 if you deposited all FUTA tax when due (S14, pp. 44–45).
- Under the Form 940 e-file program, a taxpayer may electronically file an original or an amended Form 940 (S14, p. 45).
- CPEOs must file Form 940 with Schedule R (Form 940) electronically, though the IRS may waive that requirement in some circumstances (S14, p. 45).
- Reporting agents that file Forms 940 for groups of taxpayers can file them electronically (S14, p. 45).
- FUTA taxes for household employees need not be deposited unless their wages are reported on Forms 941, 943 or 944; where employment taxes for them weren’t reported on those forms, the FUTA tax goes on Schedule H (Form 1040) (S14, pp. 44–45).
- Form 2678 does not cover FUTA taxes reportable on Form 940, except where the employer is a home care service recipient receiving services under a program run by a federal, state or local government agency (S16, p. 53).
- A CPEO files Schedule R (Form 940), Schedule R (Form 941) or Schedule R (Form 943), matching the aggregate return it files, which is Form 940, Form 941 or Form 943 respectively (S16, p. 54).
- Return transcripts for Form 940 for tax years 2023 and later can be accessed through your IRS business tax account (B04, p. 57).
