## Note
Section 9 of the publication covers withholding from employees' wages. It has three parts, Federal Income Tax Withholding, Social Security and Medicare Taxes, and Part-Time Workers, which follow in the cluster below. The chapter's own text is one caution, summarised here.

**Caution (p. 25).** Employers in American Samoa, Guam, the CNMI, the USVI and Puerto Rico can disregard the references to federal income tax withholding, unless they have employees who are subject to U.S. income tax withholding. Such employers should contact their local tax department for information about income tax withholding.

## Parts of Withholding From Employees’ Wages
- [[P15-09_Federal-Income-Tax-Withholding]] — the redesigned Form W-4, using Form W-4 to figure withholding, exemption from withholding, nonresident alien employees, IRS lock-in letters, substitute and invalid Forms W-4, and levies (pp. 25–29)
- [[P15-09_Social-Security-and-Medicare-Taxes]] — the Federal Insurance Contributions Act taxes, the farmwork tests, the 2026 rates and wage base limit, Additional Medicare Tax, successor employers and other special cases (pp. 29–31)
- [[P15-09_Part-Time-Workers]] — part-time workers and workers hired for short periods are treated as full-time employees for withholding and for social security, Medicare and FUTA taxes (p. 31)
