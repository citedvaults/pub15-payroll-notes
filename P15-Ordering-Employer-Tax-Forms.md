## Note
**Getting forms (p. 10).** Most of the forms, instructions and publications you may need can be viewed, downloaded or printed at IRS.gov/Forms. Otherwise, IRS.gov/OrderForms can be used to place an order and have them mailed to you. The IRS will process an order as soon as possible, and requests already sent should not be resubmitted. Forms, instructions and publications can be had faster online.

**Filing Forms W-2 and W-3 electronically (p. 10).** In place of ordering paper Forms W-2 and W-3, the page suggests considering the SSA’s free e-file service. To learn more about BSO, it points to the SSA’s Employer W-2 Filing Instructions & Information webpage at SSA.gov/employer. Forms W-2 can be created online and submitted to the SSA by typing wage information into fill-in fields. Completed copies of Forms W-2 can also be printed for filing with state or local governments, for giving to employees and for your records. Form W-3 is created for you from your Forms W-2.

**Puerto Rico (p. 10).** The SSA’s BSO is independent of the Government of Puerto Rico’s electronic filing system. Puerto Rico employers must go to Hacienda.pr.gov for additional information.
