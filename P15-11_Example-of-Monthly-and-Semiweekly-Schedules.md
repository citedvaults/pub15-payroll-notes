## Note
**Rose Co. (Form 941 taxes) (pp. 33–34).** The example gives Rose Co.'s Form 941 taxes for two lookback periods:
- 2025 lookback period, 3rd quarter 2023 through 2nd quarter 2024: $12,000 in each of the 4 quarters, $48,000 in all.
- 2026 lookback period, 3rd quarter 2024 through 2nd quarter 2025: $12,000 in each of 3rd quarter 2024, 4th quarter 2024 and 1st quarter 2025, and $15,000 in 2nd quarter 2025, $51,000 in all.

For 2025, Rose Co. is a monthly schedule depositor: its liability for the 4 quarters of that lookback period was not more than $50,000. For 2026 it will be a semiweekly schedule depositor, since the taxes for the 4 quarters of its lookback period exceeded $50,000 (p. 34).

**Example for employers of farmworkers (p. 34).** Red Co. reported $48,000 on line 13 of its 2024 Form 943 and $60,000 on line 13 of its 2025 Form 943. For 2026 it is a monthly schedule depositor, because its lookback period is calendar year 2024, with taxes of $48,000, which were not more than $50,000. For 2027 it will be a semiweekly schedule depositor, because its lookback period is calendar year 2025, with taxes of $60,000, which exceeded $50,000.
