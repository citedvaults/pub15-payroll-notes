## Note
Section 17 of the publication covers the certifying requirements of federal agencies for federal income taxes withheld from U.S. Government employees working in, or federal pension recipients residing in, American Samoa, the CNMI and Guam. It has three parts, Special Certifying Requirements for Federal Agencies (with a part for each territory it names), "Federal Income Taxes" From American Samoa, the CNMI, or Guam, and Certification Procedures, which follow in the clusters below. The chapter's own text is its heading alone, so nothing further is summarised here.

## Parts of Federal Agency Certifying Requirements
- [[P15-17_Special-Certifying-Requirements]] — the legal authorities requiring federal agencies to certify federal income taxes withheld, and the note that these requirements do not apply to federal agencies with employees working in Puerto Rico or the USVI (p. 54)
- [[P15-17_Taxes-From-American-Samoa-CNMI-Guam]] — which federal income taxes the certification procedures cover, listed by the group of employees, pensioners or military personnel they were withheld from (p. 55)
- [[P15-17_Certification-Procedures]] — the quarterly certification to the IRS, the contents of the letter, the annual certification by an agency that certifies for other agencies, the data required for certain servicemembers, the quarterly due dates and the mailing address (pp. 55–56)

## Parts of Special Certifying Requirements for Federal Agencies
- [[P15-17_American-Samoa]] — the Code sections requiring the U.S. Government to cover over federal income taxes withheld for residents of American Samoa to its Treasury (p. 54)
- [[P15-17_Northern-Mariana-Islands]] — the cover over requirement for the CNMI, and the Caution on the 2006 agreement under which federal employers withhold CNMI income taxes for certain employees (pp. 54–55)
- [[P15-17_Guam]] — the cover over requirement for Guam (p. 55)
- [[P15-17_Puerto-Rico]] — the note that the special certifying requirements do not apply to federal agencies with employees working in Puerto Rico, and the Caution on the 1988 agreement (p. 55)
- [[P15-17_US-Virgin-Islands]] — the note that the special certifying requirements do not apply to federal agencies with employees working in the USVI (p. 55)
