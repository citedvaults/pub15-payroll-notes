## Note
**Timely mailing as timely filing (p. 10).** Certain private delivery services (PDSs) designated by the IRS can be used to meet the “timely mailing as timely filing” rule for tax returns. IRS.gov/PDS, the page says, has the current list of PDSs, and the PDS itself can explain how to get written proof of the mailing date.

**Mailing address to use (p. 10).** For the IRS mailing address to use with a PDS, the page points to IRS.gov/PDSstreetAddresses. On that webpage, pick the address in the same state as the address to which you would mail returns filed without a payment, as given in the instructions for your employment tax return.

**P.O. boxes (p. 10).** A PDS can’t deliver to a P.O. box. For any item going to an IRS P.O. box address, you must use the U.S. Postal Service (USPS).
