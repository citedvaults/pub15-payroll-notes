## Note
The special certifying requirements do not apply to federal agencies that have employees working in Puerto Rico (p. 55).

**Caution (p. 55).** In November 1988 the U.S. Treasury Department and Puerto Rico made an agreement under 5 U.S.C. section 5517. Under it, every federal employer (the Department of Defense included) must withhold Puerto Rico income taxes in place of federal income taxes, and deposit them with the Puerto Rico Treasury, for employees whose regular place of federal employment is Puerto Rico. Federal employers must also file quarterly and annual reports with the Puerto Rico tax department. The 5517 agreement does not apply to payments to pensioners, or to pay of members of the U.S. Armed Forces who are stationed in Puerto Rico but whose state of legal residence is outside Puerto Rico. The page refers to IRS.gov/5517Agreements for more information, including details on completing Form W-2.
