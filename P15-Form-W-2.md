## Note
- For payments made after calendar year 2025, the page raises to $2,000 the threshold for wage reporting on Forms W-2, Wage and Tax Statement, when no federal income, social security or Medicare tax was withheld; the threshold will be inflation-adjusted for each year after 2026, and the page points to the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 and for Certain Information Returns (F03, p. 2).
- In its items on qualified tips and qualified overtime compensation, the page says employers and other payers file information returns (Forms W-2, 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC are examples) with the SSA or IRS as applicable and furnish statements to the recipients, and that the IRS has given transition relief for the 2025 tax year reporting requirements (F03, p. 2).
- Unless the text says otherwise, “Form W-2” in the publication takes in Forms W-2AS, W-2CM, W-2GU and W-2VI as well as Form 499R-2/W-2PR (F04, p. 3).
- For CNMI federal employers, the page points to IRS.gov/5517Agreements for more information, details on completing Form W-2 included (F04, p. 6).
- For electronic filing of Forms W-2 (the territorial forms included), the page directs you to SSA.gov/employer; you may be required to file Forms W-2 electronically, and the page points to the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (F05, p. 6).
- You must file Forms W-2 to report wages paid to employees (F08, p. 7).
- Wages and other pay to employees go on Form W-2 rather than on Forms 1099, as a rule, and the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 cover filing details and electronic filing (F08, p. 7).
- The IRS runs the TSO to answer questions about reporting on Forms W-2, W-3 and 1099 and on other information returns (F08, p. 7).
- The Employer Responsibilities list, under Annually (see Calendar for due dates), includes furnishing each employee a Form W-2, filing Copy A of Forms W-2 with the SSA along with Form W-3, and reconciling Forms 941 (or 943 or 944) with Forms W-2 and W-3 (F09, p. 8).
- Some nonqualified pension plan and deferred compensation plan distributions (those of section 457(b) plans of tax-exempt organizations included) count as wages and are reported on Form W-2 (F10, p. 8).
- Your records should include employee copies of Forms W-2 and W-2c that were returned to you undeliverable (F11, p. 9).
- In place of ordering paper Forms W-2 and W-3, the page suggests the SSA’s free e-file service, where you can create Forms W-2 online by typing wage information into fill-in fields and submit them to the SSA (F19, p. 10).
- That service also lets you print finished copies of Forms W-2 for state or local governments, for your employees and for your own records (F19, p. 10).
- Under the Calendar’s Furnish Forms 1099 and W-2 item, each employee receives a completed 2025 Form W-2 (F21, p. 11).
- Under the Calendar’s File Form W-2 item, Copy A of all 2025 Forms W-2, paper or electronic, goes to the SSA with Form W-3, Transmittal of Wage and Tax Statements (F21, p. 11).
- Forms W-2AS, W-2CM, W-2GU and W-2VI go with Form W-3SS, and Forms 499R-2/W-2PR go with Form W-3 (PR); for filing electronically with the SSA the page names the SSA’s Employer W-2 Filing Instructions & Information webpage at SSA.gov/employer, and notes that the SSA’s Form W-2 Online service generates the Form W-3 data (F21, p. 11).
- Copy 1 of Forms W-2AS, W-2CM, W-2GU and W-2VI, with Form W-3SS, goes to your local tax department at the address printed on Form W-3SS; CNMI employers should ask their local tax department how to file Copy 1 (F21, p. 11).
- For filing Forms 499R-2/W-2PR with the Puerto Rico Department of Treasury, the page gives Hacienda.pr.gov and 787-622-0123 (F21, p. 11).
- H-2A workers are not statutory employees, so box 13 (Statutory employee) of Form W-2 is not checked for them (S02, p. 15).
- Employers have to obtain each employee’s name and SSN and enter them on Form W-2, resident and nonresident alien employees included (S04, p. 17).
- A wrong employee name or SSN on Form W-2 can bring a penalty unless there is reasonable cause (S04, p. 17).
- For a paper Form W-2 where the employee has applied for an SSN but has none yet, “Applied For” is entered; for an electronic filing, the SSN field gets all zeros (S04, p. 17).
- Once the employee receives the SSN, Copy A of Form W-2c is filed with the SSA to show it, Copies B, C and 2 of Form W-2c go to the employee, and the employee should be advised to correct the SSN on the original Form W-2 (S04, p. 17).
- After the SSA issues an updated card following a name change, or a new card with a different SSN following a change in alien work status, a Form W-2c corrects the name and SSN on the most recently filed Form W-2; earlier years need no correction if the previous name and number were used for them (S04, p. 17).
- An ITIN is not used in place of an SSN on Form W-2 (S04, p. 18).
- Noncash wages paid to farmworkers go in box 1 of Form W-2 (box 7 of Form 499R-2/W-2PR) with the cash wages, and stay out of box 3 or 5 (box 20 or 22 of Form 499R-2/W-2PR) unless the transaction is in substance a cash payment (S05, p. 18).
- Pay of $600 or more in 2025 ($2,000 or more in 2026) to foreign agricultural workers on H-2A visas is reported in box 1 of Form W-2 (box 7 of Form 499R-2/W-2PR), not as social security wages (box 3) or Medicare wages (box 5), since agricultural labor under that visa isn’t subject to social security and Medicare taxes (S05, p. 19).
- For H-2A workers, box 13 (Statutory employee) is left unchecked and any federal income tax withheld goes in box 2 of Form W-2; these rules hold when the worker gives the employer a TIN (S05, p. 19).
- When a per diem or allowance exceeds the substantiated amount, the excess is wages; the substantiated (nontaxable) amount is shown in box 12 of Form W-2 with code “L,” and employers in Puerto Rico use box 12 without a code (S05, p. 20).
- Differential wage payments are wages for income tax withholding but not for social security, Medicare or FUTA tax, and they are reported in box 1 of Form W-2 (box 7 of Form 499R-2/W-2PR) (S05, p. 21).
- Where you deposited the right amount but withheld less from the employee, you may recover the social security, Medicare or income tax deposited on their behalf and included on their Form W-2, though the income tax must be recovered before April 1 of the following year (S05, p. 22).
- Identity protection services an employer provides, including those given before a data breach, are outside the employee’s gross income and need not be reported on an information return such as Form W-2 (S05, p. 22).
- Tips, and the collected and uncollected social security and Medicare taxes on them, are reported on Form W-2 (Form 499R-2/W-2PR for employers in Puerto Rico) (S06, p. 23).
- Uncollected social security tax goes in box 12 of Form W-2 with code “A” and uncollected Medicare tax with code “B”; on Form 499R-2/W-2PR they go in boxes 25 and 26 (S06, p. 23).
- Uncollected Additional Medicare Tax is kept out of box 12 of Form W-2 (S06, p. 23).
- For more on reporting tips, the page points to section 13 and to the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (S06, p. 23).
- The amount added to a nonresident alien employee’s wages only for figuring income tax withholding appears in no box of the employee’s Form W-2 and does not raise the employee’s income tax liability (S09.01, p. 27).
- The IRS relies on information reported on Form W-2 to spot employees with withholding compliance problems (S09.01, p. 28).
- A household or agricultural employer that pays the employee’s share of social security and Medicare taxes instead of withholding it adds that amount to box 1 of the employee’s Form W-2 (box 7 of Form 499R-2/W-2PR), and leaves it out of social security and Medicare wages and out of boxes 3 and 5 (S09.02, p. 30).
- In the example, Gavrielle puts $1,076.50 in box 1 of Dan’s Form W-2 ($1,000 of wages plus $76.50 of taxes paid for Dan) and $1,000.00 in boxes 3 and 5 (S09.02, p. 30).
- You meet the EIC notification requirement by issuing a Form W-2 that has the EIC notice printed on the back of Copy B, or a substitute Form W-2 containing the same statement (S10, p. 31).
- Where a substitute Form W-2 was given on time but lacks the required statement, the employee must be notified within 1 week of the date the substitute was given (S10, p. 31).
- Where Form W-2 is required but wasn’t given on time, Notice 797 or your own written statement goes to the employee by the date Form W-2 is required to be given; where no Form W-2 is required, the employee is notified by February 9, 2026 (S10, p. 31).
- In addition to civil penalties, you may face criminal prosecution for willfully furnishing false or fraudulent Forms W-2 to employees or failing to furnish Forms W-2, among other acts the page lists (S11.03, p. 37).
- An employer that must file a final return also furnishes Forms W-2 to its employees and files Forms W-2 and W-3 with the SSA, by the final return’s due date (S12, p. 39).
- Discrepancies between Forms 941, 943 or 944 filed with the IRS and Forms W-2 and W-3 filed with the SSA may lead the IRS or the SSA to contact you (S12, p. 39).
- To reduce discrepancies, bonuses are reported as wages and as social security and Medicare wages on Forms W-2 and on Forms 941, 943 or 944 (S12, p. 40).
- Social security and Medicare wages and taxes are reported separately on Forms W-2 and W-3 and on Forms 941, 943 or 944 (S12, p. 40).
- The employee share of social security tax belongs in the Form W-2 box for social security tax withheld (box 4), not in social security wages, and the employee share of Medicare tax in the box for Medicare tax withheld (box 6), not in Medicare wages (S12, p. 40).
- On Form 499R-2/W-2PR, social security tax withheld is in box 21 and Medicare tax withheld in box 23 (S12, p. 40).
- No employee’s social security wages may exceed the annual social security wage base limit ($184,500 for 2026) (S12, p. 40).
- Noncash wages that are not subject to social security or Medicare tax are not reported as social security or Medicare wages (S12, p. 40).
- The amounts on Form W-3 must total the amounts from the Forms W-2 (S12, p. 40).
- Only taxes and withholding that appear on Form W-2 belong on Forms 941, 943 or 944, while nonpayroll withholding on Forms 1099 or Form W-2G goes on Form 945 (S12, p. 40).
- Amounts on Forms W-2, W-3 and Forms 941, 943 or 944 can differ for valid reasons (S12, p. 40).
- For how Form W-2 reports the employee’s share of social security and Medicare taxes left uncollected on tips and on group-term life insurance, the page points to the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (S13.01, p. 41).
- An income tax withholding error can be adjusted only if found in the same calendar year the wages were paid, since the employee takes the amount on Form W-2 (or Form W-2c) as a credit on the income tax return (S13.02, p. 42).
- An administrative error adjustment brings the amount on Form 941, 943 or 944 into line with what was actually withheld and shown on the employees’ Forms W-2 (S13.02, p. 42).
- Under the heading Correcting filed Forms W-2 and W-3, the page says a changed wage total for a previous year, which leads to adjusting wages and social security and Medicare taxes, also means filing Form W-2c and Form W-3c with the SSA; online sessions may include up to 25 Forms W-2c per Form W-3c, with no limit on sessions (S13.02, p. 43).
- In the section’s table, the row on wages paid to a beneficiary or estate in the same calendar year as the worker’s death points to the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 for details (S15, p. 47).
- In the table’s row on election workers for state and local governments, a note says Form W-2 is to be filed on payments of $600 or more in 2025 ($2,000 or more in 2026), whether or not social security or Medicare tax was withheld (S15, p. 49).
- A payroll service provider (PSP) may prepare Forms W-2, and so may an agent authorized under section 3504 (S16, p. 53).
- Where an employer and a section 3504 authorized agent (or CPEO or other third-party payer) both paid wages to an employee in a quarter, each should issue Forms W-2 for the wages it paid that employee during the year (S16, p. 54).
- For CNMI and Puerto Rico federal employers under the 5517 agreement, the page points to IRS.gov/5517Agreements for more information, details on completing Form W-2 included (B01.02, p. 55; B01.04, p. 55).
- The SSA offers an online service at SSA.gov/employer, described as fast, free and secure, with W-2 filing options for CPAs, accountants, enrolled agents and individuals who process Form W-2 and Form W-2c (B04, p. 57).
