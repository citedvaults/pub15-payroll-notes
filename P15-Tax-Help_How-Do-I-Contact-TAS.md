## Note
TAS has offices in every state, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico (p. 58). The page gives three ways to find your local advocate's number (p. 58):
- go to www.TaxpayerAdvocate.IRS.gov/Contact-Us;
- check your local directory; or
- call TAS toll free at 877-777-4778.
