## Note
**What you must file (p. 7).** Wages paid to employees are reported by filing Forms W-2. Certain other payments made during the year may also require information returns. As an example, Form 1099-NEC, Nonemployee Compensation, must be filed for payments to persons not treated as employees (independent contractors, for instance) for services performed for your trade or business: $600 or more if paid in 2025, and $2,000 or more if paid in 2026.

**Where the details are (p. 7).** Details on filing Forms 1099 for payments made in 2025, and on required electronic filing, are in the General Instructions for Certain Information Returns and in the separate, specific instructions for each information return you file; the Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC are one example. The page says a new Pub. 1099 will replace those general instructions for payments made after 2025. Details on filing Form W-2, and on its required electronic filing, are in the General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3.

**Forms 1099 and wages (p. 7).** Generally, wages and other compensation paid to employees are reported on Form W-2, not on Forms 1099.

**Technical Services Operation (TSO) (p. 7).** The IRS operates the TSO to answer questions on reporting on Forms W-2, W-3 and 1099 and other information returns. Questions can be put by phone to 866-455-7438 (toll free), 304-263-8700 (toll call) or 304-579-4827 (TDD), or by email to mccirp@irs.gov. Taxpayer identification numbers (TINs) and attachments should not go in an email, because email isn’t secure.
