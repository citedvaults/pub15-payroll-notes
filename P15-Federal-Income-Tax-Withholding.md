## Note
**Caution (p. 7).** Employers in American Samoa, Guam, the CNMI, the USVI and Puerto Rico can disregard the references to federal income tax withholding, unless they have employees who are subject to U.S. income tax withholding. For information about income tax withholding they should contact their local tax department.

**What to withhold on (pp. 7–8).** Federal income tax is withheld from each wage payment or supplemental unemployment compensation plan benefit payment, based on the employee’s Form W-4 and on the correct table in Pub. 15-T. Farm operators and crew leaders must also withhold federal income tax from farmworkers’ wages when those wages are subject to social security and Medicare taxes. For supplemental wages paid to an employee, the page refers to section 7. For nonresident alien employees, it refers to Withholding federal income taxes on the wages of nonresident alien employees in section 9. For withholding on pensions (including distributions from tax-favored retirement plans), annuities and individual retirement arrangements (IRAs), it refers to section 8 of Pub. 15-A.

**Employer Responsibilities (p. 8).** The page gives a list that briefly summarises your basic responsibilities. Individual circumstances can vary greatly, so responsibilities for withholding, depositing and reporting employment taxes can differ. Each item carries a page reference to a more detailed discussion in the publication, and those pages are given below.

*New employees:*
- Verify work eligibility of new employees (page 7).
- Record employees’ names and SSNs from social security cards (page 7).
- Ask employees for Form W-4 (page 7).

*Each payday:*
- Withhold federal income tax based on each employee’s Form W-4 (page 25).
- Withhold the employee’s share of social security and Medicare taxes (page 29).
- Deposit the withheld federal income tax, the withheld and employer social security taxes, and the withheld and employer Medicare taxes (page 31). How soon a deposit is due generally depends on whether you follow the monthly or the semiweekly deposit schedule.

*Quarterly (by April 30, July 31, October 31 and January 31):*
- Deposit FUTA tax if the undeposited amount is over $500 (page 44).
- File Form 941, paying the tax with the return if not required to deposit (page 38).

*Annually (see Calendar for due dates):*
- File Form 943 if required, paying the tax with the return if not required to deposit (page 38).
- File Form 944 if required, paying the tax with the return if not required to deposit (page 38).
- Remind employees to submit a new Form W-4 if they need to change their federal income tax withholding (page 25).
- Ask employees who claim exemption from federal income tax withholding for a new Form W-4 (page 27).
- Reconcile Forms 941 (or Form 943 or Form 944) with Forms W-2 and W-3 (page 39).
- Furnish each employee a Form W-2 (page 11).
- File Copy A of Forms W-2 and the transmittal Form W-3 with the SSA (page 11).
- Furnish each payee a Form 1099, for example Form 1099-NEC (page 11).
- File Forms 1099 and the transmittal Form 1096 (page 11).
- File Form 940 (page 11).
- File Form 945 for any nonpayroll income tax withholding (page 11).
